Our Origin & Vibe

"Coffee House Meets Collegiate Commons."

Katowice is a university city buzzing with ideas, deadlines, and international minds. El Shaddai was born out of a simple need: students and academics needed a warm, unhurried space that serves real, soulful South Indian decoction coffee, alongside an ultra-dependable print desk where you can bind your master's thesis or photocopy seminar readings without running across town.

Whether you are an Erasmus scholar homesick for ginger chai, an academic preparing a lecture on Jagiellońska street, or a local looking for crispy handmade samosas and roasted chicory aromas, our doors are open from early morning until dusk.
